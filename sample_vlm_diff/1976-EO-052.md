## VLM

OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 52
FEBRUARY 26, 1976
AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 11
DATED MARCH 14, 1974
Whereas, It is necessary and desirable to amend Executive Order No. 11, outlining the duties and functions of the Criminal Justice Coordinating Council; and

Whereas, It is necessary and desirable to separate the positions of Chairman and Director of the Council to insure more efficient operation in order to better carry out these functions and duties.
Now, therefore, by the power vested in me as Mayor of The City of New York, it is hereby ordered as follows:

Section 1. Section 2 of Executive Order No. 11 dated March 14, 1974, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"2. The Mayor shall appoint a Chairman of the Council who shall serve at the pleasure of the Mayor. The Mayor shall also appoint a Director of the Council who shall serve at the pleasure of the Mayor and carry out such duties, functions and responsibilities as may be prescribed by the Chairman."
§ 2. This order shall take effect immediately.

ABRAHAM D. BEAME, Mayor.
