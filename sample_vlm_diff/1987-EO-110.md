## VLM

```
OFFICE OF
THE MAYOR
Executive Order No. 110
February 23, 1987
ESTABLISHMENT OF THE MAYOR'S OFFICE FOR HOMELESS
AND SINGLE ROOM OCCUPANCY (SRO) HOUSING SERVICES
By virtue of the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of
New York, it is hereby ordered:
Section 1. Office of Director for Homeless and Single Room
Occupancy Housing Services Established. There is established in
the Office of the Mayor the Office for Homeless and Single Room
Occupancy Services, headed by the Director for Homeless and Single
Room Occupancy Housing Services (the "Director").
Section 2. Responsibilities of the Director. The Director
shall, consistent with law and in consultation and together with
the Department of Housing Preservation and Development (HPD), the
Human Resources Administration (HRA) and such other agencies as
the Mayor may direct, recommend policies, procedures and
guidelines, with respect to social, health, employment, shelter
and other services for the homeless and for Single Room Occupancy
(SRO) tenants; and coordinate and facilitate housing for the
homeless and SRO tenants, including, but not limited to, assisting
the agencies primarily responsible for:
(a) the selection and evaluation of sites for shelters
and other facilities for the homeless;
(b) the format, content, and level of detail required
for bids and Request for Proposals, and, in
conjunction with the Corporation Counsel, contract
terms and conditions;
(c) the design and implementation of inter-agency
programs and services.
Section 3. The Director and such other agencies as the Mayor
may direct shall administer an SRO loan program and shall develop
SRO housing options to preserve and expand the number of SRO
housing units,
```
