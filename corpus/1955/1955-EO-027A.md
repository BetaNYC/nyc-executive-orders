# CITY OF NEW YORK

OFFICE OF THE MAYOR

NEW YORK 7, N. Y.

October 26, 1955 gdp

## MEMORANDUM

TO: ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

FROM: THE DEPUTY MAYOR

Reference is made to the Mayor's Executive Order #25 dated August 2, 1955, which outlined the procedure to be followed in submitting proposed State legislation to be included in the City's 1956 legislative program, and established October 15th, as the date for submitting such bills for consideration by this office.

The purpose of this memorandum is to again call the matter to your attention for appropriate action. In cases where the October date was not met, you are requested to notify Victor F. Condello, Assistant to the Mayor, of any legislative proposals presently under consideration by your agency, and the estimated date by which they will be submitted to this office.

It is further requested that departments and agencies not contemplating the recommendation of any State legislation, so notify Mr. Condello.

HENRY EPSTEIN
DEPUTY MAYOR
