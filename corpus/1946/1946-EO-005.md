# CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
C
O
P
Y
February 7, 1946
MEMORANDUM #5
To: ALL DEPARTMENT MEADS
From: THE MAYOR
When the fuel oil in all public buildings over which you have supervision declines to a three-day supply, you will immediately close these buildings in order that the remaining quantity of fuel may be used to keep the pipes from bursting through freezing.
This does not apply to buildings essential to public health and safety.
