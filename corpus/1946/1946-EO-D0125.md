Under date of January 10, 1946, the Mayor transmitted to all heads of departments Memorandum #2 requesting the preparation of an organization chart for each department along the lines indicated in the memorandum. This organization chart should have been received at the office of the Mayor not later than January 18th.
It has now come to my attention that some of the departments claim that the memorandum was not received by them. I am therefore enclosing another copy of Memorandum #2. The Mayor would appreciate it if all organization charts were in his hands not later than Friday, February 1, 1946. Please have the charts directed to my attention.
PAUL L. ROSS
Administrative Secretary to the Mayor
