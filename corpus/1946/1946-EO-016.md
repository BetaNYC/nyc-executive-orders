December 31, 1946.

MEMORANDUM NO. 16

TO: HEADS OF ALL MAYOR'S DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

Early in 1946 the heads of all City Departments received oral instructions from me to clear all matters of public relations in which administration policy was involved with William J. Donoghue, Executive Secretary to the Mayor.

The order is hereby renewed.
