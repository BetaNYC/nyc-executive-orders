THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007

EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 2

January 1, 1994

DEPUTY MAYORS

By the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, it is hereby ordered:

Section 1. There shall be four Deputy Mayors. One shall be designated the Deputy Mayor for Operations, one shall be designated the Deputy Mayor for Education and Human Services, one shall be designated the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Community Relations, and one shall be designated the Deputy Mayor for Finance and Economic Development.
