THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 6
April 8, 1994
COMMISSION FOR THE UNITED NATIONS AND FOR THE CONSULAR CORPS
(Amendment of Executive Order No. 7, dated April 10, 1970)
By the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, It is hereby
ordered:
Section 1. Prior Order Amended. Section 2 of Executive Order No. 7, dated April 10, 1970, is amended to read as follows:
§2. The Commission shall be headed by one or more Commissioners for the United Nations and for the Consular Corps. The Commissioners shall serve without salary and at the pleasure of the Mayor, and be the Mayor's personal representatives.
Section 2. Section 3 of Executive Order No. 7, dated April 10, 1970, is amended by adding a new subdivision (f) to read as follows:
(f) Working with the Consular Corps and the international business community to assist in the enhancement of New York City's role in international trade and commerce.
Section 3. Effective Date. This Order shall take effect immediately.
