# MEMORANDUM
July 9, 1968
TO: All Administrators, Commissioners, Heads of Agencies and Assistants to the Mayor
Sheldon Brosoff is the new Director of the Mayor's Action Center. Please instruct your personnel to give their utmost cooperation to him and his staff whenever the need arises.
Please have forwarded to Sheldon Brosoff, Room 8, City Hall, the names and telephone numbers of the individuals designated by you to serve as a liaison between your agency and the Mayor's Action Center.
