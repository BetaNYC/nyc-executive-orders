CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N. Y. 10007

EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 89

August 12, 1968

## Administration of Budget Appropriation
Regarding State, Federal or Other Grants

WHEREAS, the New York City Charter empowers and charges the Mayor with the responsibility of administering the Expense Budget for the 1968-69 fiscal year, and

WHEREAS, it is necessary to provide for administration and control of applications for state, federal or other grants.

NOW, THEREFORE, by the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, it is hereby ordered:

Section 1. Any agency making an application for a State, Federal, Foundation or other grant shall submit a copy of such application to the Bureau of the Budget at the time the application is initiated. When the agency receives approval of such grant in the original or amended form, the agency shall notify the Bureau of the Budget by letter of such approval.

2. This order shall take effect immediately.
