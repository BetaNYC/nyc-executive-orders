CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N. Y. 10007

EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 66

February 19, 1968

## DECLARATION TERMINATING HEALTH EMERGENCY

WHEREAS, on February 8, 1968, by reason of a strike of the employees of the Department of Sanitation of the City of New York, I ordered that "A HEALTH EMERGENCY is hereby deemed to exist"; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Health of the City of New York advised me that such state of emergency is no longer necessary to assure the maintenance of the public health and safety and the prevention of disease; and

WHEREAS, the striking employees of the Department of Sanitation have returned to work and arrangements have been made by the City and the Union of said employees for the final resolution of all the matters in dispute;

NOW, THEREFORE, by the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, I hereby order that the Health Emergency established by me on February 8, 1968, is hereby terminated.

John V. Lindsay
Mayor
