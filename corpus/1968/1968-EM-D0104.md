# EXECUTIVE MEMORANDA

January 4, 1968- By Harry J. O'Donnell, Press Secretary.

Information-type and special telephone numbers for use by public; requesting City agencies establishing same to contact Mr. Philip C. Hitchins, New York Telephone Company Account Manager for the City.

April 12, 1968- By John V. Lindsay, Mayor.

Modification of Executive Order No. 5, dated January 19, 1966 and Executive Memorandum Instructions issued Feb. 2, 1967 relating to positions, the salary of which is $13,000 or more a year.
