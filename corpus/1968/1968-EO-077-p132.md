Executive Order No. 77
(2) section six of this executive order shall not take effect unless a 1968 bill entitled "An Act to amend the administrative code of the city of New York, and the education law, in relation to providing for or authorizing pension-providing-for-increased-take-home-pay, death benefits and reductions in member retirement contributions with respect to members and other persons entitled to benefits under the teachers' retirement system and the retirement system of the board of education of the city of New York" is enacted into law.
JOHN V. LINDSAY
MAYOR
BY ROBERT W. SWEET
Deputy Mayor
