THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 34
(Amends E.O. No. 26, dated January 11, 1996 and E.O. No. 82, dated April 26, 1985)
December 6 1996
SUPERVISION AND MANAGEMENT OF CHILD DAY
CARE SERVICES BY THE ADMINISTRATION FOR
CHILDREN'S SERVICES
Section 1. Subdivision 2 of section 2 of Executive Order No. 26, dated
January 11, 1996, and sections 3, 5, and 7 of such Order are amended to read as follows:
Section 2. Powers and Duties of Commissioner. The AFCS Commissioner/Special Adviser to the Mayor on Children's Services, to the maximum extent consistent with existing law, shall be responsible for:
2. Directing and supervising the management, operations, budget and funding of the OCSE, the Head Start Program and Child Day Care Services;
Section 3. Office of Child Enforcement, the Head Start Program and Child Day Care Services.
The City's OCSE, the Head Start Program and Child Day Care Services formerly administered by the Agency for Child Development of HRA shall be continued, and shall hereafter be maintained under the supervision and direction of the AFCS Commissioner.

Dec 6 '96 18:04
Section 5. **Budgeting**. The AFCS Commissioner, subject to the supervision of the Mayor, shall work directly with the City's Office of Management and Budget with regard to the funding and administration of the City's budgets for the AFCS, the OCSE, the Head Start Program, and for Child Day Care Services units of AFCS.
Section 7. **Coordinated Administration with HRA**. The AFCS, OCSE, the Head Start Program and Child Day Care Services units of AFCS, to the extent that the AFCS Commissioner determines it is appropriate, shall continue to have the benefit of, and access to all legal, administrative and support services currently provided to them by HRA, and HRA shall continue to provide such services to the AFCS, and OCSE, the Head Start Program and Child Day Care Services units of AFCS.
Section 2. Section 1 of Executive Order No. 82, dated April 26, 1985, as amended by Executive Order No. 26 of January 11, 1996, is amended to read as follows:
Section 1. **Human Resources Administration**. There hereby is established in the Executive Office of the Mayor a Human Resources Administration ("Administration") with the responsibility to establish, coordinate and implement City policies with regard to social service, human resources and community development programs currently supervised by the Administration. The Administration will not be responsible for the Administration for Children's Services ("AFCS"), the Office of child Support Enforcement ("OCSE"), the Head Start Program and child Day Care Services. The Administration shall, however, provide legal, administrative and support services to the AFCS, the OCSE, the Head Start Program and Child Day Care Services of AFCS upon request of the AFCS Commissioner.
Section 3. This Order shall take effect on the date hereof, and the Administration for Children's Services and HRA shall take all steps necessary for the Administration for Children's Services to assume management and supervisory responsibility for Child Day Care Services consistent with the terms of this Order.
Rudolph W. Giuliani
Mayor
