THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007

August 18, 1970

# MEMORANDUM

TO: ALL AGENCY HEADS

FROM: RICHARD R. AURELIO

RE: SECTION 1100 OF THE CITY CHARTER

I would like to direct your attention to Section 1100 of the New York City Charter which states that "every head of an administration or department... who receives a salary from the City shall not engage in any other occupation, profession or employment."
