THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 46
MARCH 13, 1980
CONCESSIONS REVIEW COMMITTEE
By the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, it is
hereby ordered:
Section 1. Prior Executive Order. Section 2 of Executive Order
No. 9, dated March 16, 1978, is hereby amended to read as follows:
82. A Concessions Review Committee (hereinafter called the Committee) is hereby created in the Office of the Mayor. The Committee shall consist of the following officials or their designees: The Mayor, the Director of the Office of Management and Budget, the Corporation Counsel, and, if they consent to serve, the Comptroller, the Vice Chairman of the City Council, and the President of the New York City Public Development Corporation.
82. Effective Date. This Order shall take effect immediately.
MAYOR
