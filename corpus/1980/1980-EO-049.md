OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 49
APRIL 24, 1980
CETA TRANSITION
By the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, it is hereby
ordered:
Section 1. Prior Order Amended. Section 4 of Executive Order No. 31,
dated July 20, 1979, is hereby amended in part to read as follows:
"g 4. Reports of Agency Heads. Agency heads will report on a quarterly basis to the Deputy Mayor for Operations on the steps taken in furtherance of this Executive Order. The Quarterly Summary Report will be due from each agency no more than ten (10) days after the end of each quarter."
g 2. Effective Date. This order shall take effect immediately.
EDWARD I. KOCH
MAYOR
