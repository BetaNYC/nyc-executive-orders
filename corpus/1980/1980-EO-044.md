EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 44
February 13, 1980
MAYOR'S COMMITTEE ON CITY MARSHALS
By the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, it
is hereby ordered:
Section 1. Committee Established. The Mayor's Committee on
City Marshals (hereinafter called the Committee is hereby established
in the Office of the Mayor.
Section 2. Functions. The Committee shall:
(a) Establish and publish qualifying criteria for
appointment to the office of City Marshal;
(b) Recruit and receive from any source the names and
candidates for the office of City Marsha;
(c) Consider all relevant information to determine
which of the fully qualified candidates are best qualified to serve
as City marshals; and
(d) Recommend up to three qualified persons for
appointment to the office of City marshal upon the occurrence of a
vacancy in such office.
Section 3. Background and Financial Resources Information.
All persons to be recommended to the Mayor for appointment to the
office of City marshal shall provide the Department of Investigation
with such information relating to their background and financial
resources as the Department may require.
Section 4. Recommendations by the Committee. The Committee
shall not recommend to the Mayor any person for appointment or
reappointment to the office of City Marchal unless:
(a) The Committee has received a report on such
person's background and financial resources from the Department of
Investigation; and
(b) In the case of a reappointment, the Committee
has reviewed such person's record of performance in office and has
determined it to be satisfactory.
Section 5. Appointment by the Mayor.
(a) The Mayor shall not appoint or reappoint any
person to the office of City marshal unless such person was recommended by the Committee on City Marshals; and
(b) The Mayor shall not appoint any person to the
office of City marshal unless such person has satisfactorily completed such training as the Department of Investigation may require.
Section 6. Committee Membership.
(a) The committee shall consist of fifteen members,
and shall designate the Chairman. The presiding justices of the
Appellate Divisions of the Supreme Court for the First and Second
Judicial Departments shall each select three members. Three deans
of law schools located within the City of New York shall each select
a member. Performance of this function shall be rotated annually
among such eligible deans.
(b) The members of the Committee selected by the
Mayor and the presiding justices shall serve for a term concurrent
with the Mayor's term of office. The members selected by the law
school deans shall serve a term of one year.
(c) Vacancies in the Committee shall be filled in
the same manner as initial appointments.
(d) Members of the Committee shall be selected with due consideration for broad community representation. The membership shall
include men and women, lawyers and non-lawyers, and members of
minority and other groups who are qualified to perform the functions
of the committee.
Section 7 Confidentiality. Committee proceedings and all
applications, correspondence, considerations, interviews, transcripts,
reports, conclusions and all other records of the Committee shall
be confidential and exempt from public disclosure and shall not be
disclosed to any person other than the Mayor or his designee.
Section 8 Committee Action and Procedure. The Committee may adopt
such procedures and policies as it may deem necessary and appropriate,
but the proper discharge of its functions, including qualifying for
tenure for appointment to the office of City marshal, is outside the
control of the Committee.
Section 9. Financing.
(a) Members of the Committee shall receive no commission for their service as members.
(b) Members of the Committee shall be reimbursed for
necessary expenses incurred in connection with the responsibilities
of the Committee.
Section 10. Effective Date. This Order shall take effect
immediately.
EDWARD I. KOCH, MAYOR.
