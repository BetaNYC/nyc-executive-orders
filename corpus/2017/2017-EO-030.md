THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N. Y. 10007

EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 30

September 8, 2017

MAYORAL ADVISORY COMMISSION ON CITY ART, MONUMENTS, AND MARKERS

WHEREAS, it is necessary and desirable that the City of New York consider how to address issues relating to City-owned public art, historic monuments, and markers.

NOW THEREFORE, by the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York it is hereby ordered:

§1. Establishment of the Mayoral Advisory Commission on City Art, Monuments, and Markers. There is hereby established under the direction of the Office of the Mayor an Advisory Commission on City Art, Monuments, and Markers (hereinafter the "Commission").

(a) The Commission shall consist of two Co-Chairs and no fewer than 10 members with widely recognized expertise in relevant disciplines, and officials from relevant City agencies who will provide technical expertise and guidance, all of whom shall be appointed by the Mayor and serve at the pleasure of the Mayor. Such City officials may designate employees of their agencies to serve in their place as appropriate.

§2. Duties of the Commission. The Commission shall:

(a) Serve as an advisory group to the Mayor by proposing non-binding recommendations that may include proposed principles for the City's public works collection; policies and actions the City may consider when reviewing and addressing City works; and additional initiatives to advance positive change and further City goals.

(b) The Commission shall issue its recommendations within 90 days.

§3. Powers of the Commission.

(a) The Co-Chairs are authorized to convene meetings and establish such committees of the Commission as may be deemed necessary for the performance and carrying out of its duties.

(b) All agencies of the City of New York shall cooperate with the Commission in the performance of its duties.

§4. Effective Date. This order shall take effect immediately

Bill de Blasio
Mayor
