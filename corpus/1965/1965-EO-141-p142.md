EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 138 - ADDENDUM No. 4

TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR

SUBJECT: DISQUALIFICATION OF CERTAIN CONTRACTING FIRMS

Official notification has been received from the District Attorney of New York County that the following person after signing a waiver of immunity gave some testimony but then refused to answer further relevant questions before the Grand Jury of New York County:

Eli Smith, of Smith Decorating Co., Inc.,
327 Canal Place, Bronx, New York

In accordance with the policy set forth in Executive Order No. 138 all City Departments and Agencies are directed to take the necessary appropriate action immediately.
