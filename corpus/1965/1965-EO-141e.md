EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 139
TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR
SUBJECT: AMENDMENT TO EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 137, SUBJECT:
WATER CONSERVATION PROGRAM
Executive Order No. 137, dated April 7, 1965 is amended as follows:
Provision 10 is hereby amended to allow those who celebrate the Sabbath on Saturday to water their lawns on Sunday between the hours of 6 A.M and 9 A.M. and 8 P.M. and 11 P.M.
FOR THE MAYOR:
Edw. F. Cavanagh, Jr.
Deputy Mayor
