CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.
July 23, 1965
## EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 167
TO: American Museum of Natural History, New York Zoological Garden and Brooklyn Botanic Garden

FROM: Robert F. Wagner, Mayor

SUBJECT: Continuation of Salary Adjustment Plan for certain positions in the Cultural Institutions.
Executive Order No. 155, dated June 24, 1965 is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:
This salary adjustment plan is also continued for employees in the American Museum of Natural History, New York Zoological Garden and Brooklyn Botanic Garden whose wages are fixed in accordance with Section 202 of the Labor Law under the same terms and conditions provided by Executive Order No. 74.
ROBERT F. WAGNER
MAYOR
