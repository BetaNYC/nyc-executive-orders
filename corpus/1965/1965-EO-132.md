January 26, 1965
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 132
TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR
SUBJECT: REMOVAL OF CERTAIN CONTRACTING FIRMS FROM LIST OF DISQUALIFIED BIDDERS
Executive Memoranda Nos. 93 and 93-S, dated June 5, 1961 and June 15, 1961, respectively, listed contracting firms the bide of which were not to be considered until further notice.
The following names are hereby removed from the list of disqualified bidders:
Cartmel-Morris, Inc.
Endres Plumbing Corp.
A. Rosen & Sons
C. K. Rehner, Inc.
Forsythe Plumbing and Heating Corp.
Robert F. Wagner
Mayor
