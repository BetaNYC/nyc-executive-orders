THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007
## EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 60
MAY 25, 1972
AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 49
(October 1, 1971)
WHEREAS, it is necessary and desirable to amend Executive Order No. 49.
NOW, THEREFORE, by the power vested in me as Mayor of The City of New York, it is hereby ordered as follows:
Section 1. Executive Order No. 49, dated October 1, 1971, entitled "CONDUCT OF CENSUS CONCERNING THE COMPOSITION OF THE WORK FORCE OF CITY AGENCIES" is hereby amended by renumbering § 8 to § 9 (and by inserting therein a new § 8 to read as follows:
"§ 8. Nothing in this order shall be construed to require or allow the imposition of quotas in recruitment, assignment, hiring, or promotion in contravention of the requirements of law."
§ 2. This order shall take effect immediately.
JOHN V. LINDSAY
MAYOR
