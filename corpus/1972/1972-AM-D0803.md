# MEMORANDUM

August 3, 1972

TO: HEADS OF ALL ADMINISTRATIONS AND DEPARTMENTS

FROM: MAYOR JOHN V. LINDSAY

RE: THE UNITED FUND OF GREATER NEW YORK

Once each year municipal employees, like all people to work in New York City, have the opportunity of helping support the 425 voluntary hospital, health and welfare agencies of the United Fund of Greater New York.

These agencies are dealing with critical problems in the lives of all of our people - drug addiction, cancer, heart disease, adoption, day care, blindness, hospital care and many, many others. They serve over 1,000,000 of our fellow New Yorkers each year at a total operating cost of over one billion dollars.

For recognition of the vital role of the Fund in New York City, we have adopted a year-round voluntary giving plan so that City employees - by a modest gift each pay day - can contribute their "fair share" to benefit the entire City.

President Beame fully supports this effort and is treating the necessary procedures for smooth and efficient operation of this contribution program.
