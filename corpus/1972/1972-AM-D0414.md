# THE CITY OF NEW YORK

# ADMINISTRATIVE MEMORANDUM

April 14, 1972

HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

EDWARD A. MORRISON, DEPUTY MAYOR-CITY ADMINISTRATOR

Official notification has been received from the Commissioner
Investigation that on March 24, 1972, MR. JULIO T. MULKAY of
1291 Lathrop Avenue, Staten Island, New York, refused to answer
questions relevant to certain transactions being investigated by
Department of Investigation.

Mr. Mulkay is an officer of the J.T. Mulkay Construction
Corporation, whose principal place of business is at 1571 Lexington
Avenue, New York, New York 10029.

All contracts with the above firm or with any firm, partner-ship or corporation of which Julio T. Mulkay is a member, partner,
director or officer may be cancelled or terminated, subject only
payment of any monies owing for goods delivered or work done
prior to such cancellation or termination.

In the event the above-named person or firm submit bids for
any contract to be awarded by a City agency, the name of such
bidder should be referred to the Board of Responsibility for
determination of such bidder's responsibility in accordance with
5(c) of the Resolution of the Board of Estimate adopted on
June 30, 1949 (Cal. No. 3180A), as amended.

Requests for such a meeting of the Board of Responsibility
should be addressed to Deputy Comptroller Joseph J. Perrini, and
opinions thereof forwarded to the Corporation Counsel and the
Commissioner of Investigation.

Edward A. Morrison
Deputy Mayor-City Administrator
