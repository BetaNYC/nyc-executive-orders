# THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
OFFICE OF ADMINISTRATION
TEL. 566-6767
April 19, 1972
## ADMINISTRATIVE MEMORANDUM
TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: EDWARD A. MORRISON, DEPUTY MAYOR-CITY ADMINISTRATOR
On March 27, 1972, this Office notified you that you should refrain from entering into new contracts or extensions or modifications of existing contracts with Deltown Foods, Inc., until it has satisfied this Office that it has established and will carry out personnel and employment policies in compliance with Executive Order No. 71.
Please be advised that Deltown Foods, Inc., has now satisfied this Office that it will establish and carry out satisfactory personnel and employment policies.
Accordingly, the directions in my letter of March 27, 1972 are hereby revoked.
Edward A. Morrison
Deputy Mayor-City Administrator
