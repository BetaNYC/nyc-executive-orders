# CITY OF NEW YORK

OFFICE OF THE MAYOR

NEW YORK 7, N. Y.

June 30, 1954

TO: Heads of all Departments and Agencies

FROM: Hon. Robert F. Wagner, Mayor

SUBJECT: EXECUTIVE ORDER #13

The Department of Commerce and the Mayor's Reception Committee, in the Office of the Mayor, are hereby consolidated into a single division to be known as the Department of Commerce and Public Events. This order shall take effect July 1, 1954.
