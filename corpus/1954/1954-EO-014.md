# CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N. Y.
November 9, 1954
TO: Heads of all Departments and Agencies
FROM: Hon. Robert F. Wagner, Mayor
SUBJECT: EXECUTIVE ORDER #14
Veterans of the Korean War now in the employ of the City
are to be extended the same holiday privileges on Armistice Day
as are granted by law to veterans of World War I and II.
The existing state law (Section 63 of the Public Officers
Law) accords such privileges only to veterans who "served in the
Army or the Navy or Marine Corps of the United States . . . during
World War I or during World War II". This order is designed to
remedy this omission so that veterans of our armed forces who
served in the Korean War need not be denied Armistice Day holiday
privileges until the state law is properly amended.
