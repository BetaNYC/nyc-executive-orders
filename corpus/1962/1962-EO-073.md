CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 73
TO: Offices of the Borough Presidents, Comptroller, City Council, Board of Higher Education, New York City Community College of applied Arts and Sciences, Board of Elections Office of the District Attorneys Office of Probation, Departments of Health, Hospitals, Correction and the Courts
FROM: Robert F. Wagner, Mayor
SUBJECT: Amendment to Executive Order No. 60
The following addition is to be added after the word "agency" in the first paragraph.
Any changes in appropriations by transferring
Other Than Personal Service Funds to Personal
Service or vice-versa shall be done only upon approval
of the Mayor.
