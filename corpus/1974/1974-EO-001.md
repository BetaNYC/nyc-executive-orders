OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 1
PURSUANT TO THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION THREE OF THE NEW YORK CITY CHARTER AND EXCEPT AS HERE AFTER PROVIDED:
1. All Executive Orders in effect on December 31, 1973, are hereby continued.
2. All Executive Orders in effect on December 31, 1973, designating by name officials holding office in the government of the City to perform functions set forth in such Executive Orders are hereby modified to the extent that such functions shall be performed by the persons holding such offices pursuant to my direction.
j7-12
ABRAHAM D. BEAME, Mayor.
