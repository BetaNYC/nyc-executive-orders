OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 7

MARCH 6, 1978

BY THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS MAYOR OF THE CITY OF NEW YORK,
it is hereby ordered:

Section 1. The Model Cities Administration in the Office of the Mayor is hereby renamed and shall hereinafter be known as the Mayor's Office of Model Cities.

§ 2. The position of Model Cities Administrator shall hereinafter be known as the Director of Model Cities.

§ 3. The Director shall be appointed by the Mayor and serve at his pleasure.

§ 4. This Order shall take effect immediately.

m15-21

EDWARD I. KOCH, Mayor.
