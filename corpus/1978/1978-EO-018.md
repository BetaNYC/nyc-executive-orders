OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 18
AUGUST 29, 1978
PURSUANT TO THE POWER VESTED IN ME AS MAYOR IT IS HEREBY
ordered that:
Section 1. To conform with Section 8-0117 of the New York State Environmental Conservation Law as amended by Chapter 460 of the laws of 1978, references to "September 1, 1978" in the sixth paragraph of the Preamble to and in Section 4(c) of Executive Order No. 91, dated August 24, 1977, are hereby amended to read "November 1, 1978."
a31-s7
EDWARD I. KOCH, Mayor.
