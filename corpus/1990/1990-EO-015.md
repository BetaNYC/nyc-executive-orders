THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 15
August 15, 1990
Revocation of Executive Order No. 111
(Dated March 20, 1987). Which
Established an Audit Committee for the
City of New York.
WHEREAS, the Audit Committee for the City of New York ("Audit Committee") was originally established and its functions were set forth in Executive Order No. 29, dated February 6, 1979; and
WHEREAS, the Audit Committee was continued by Executive Order No. 111, dated March 20, 1987, which revoked Executive Order No. 29; and
WHEREAS, Executive Order No. 111 specifies that the Audit Committee shall consist of certain City officers and a certain number of private members with specified qualifications; and
WHEREAS, the recent Charter revision codified the Audit Committee as part of the New York City Charter and set forth in the Charter the qualifications for the members of the Committee.

By the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, it is hereby ordered:
Section 1. Prior Order Revoked. Executive Order No. 111 of March 20, 1987 is hereby revoked and the Audit Committee is continued as set forth in Charter 1987.
Section 2. Effective Date. This Order shall take effect immediately.
