EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 181
TO: HEADS OF ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
## THE CITY OF NEW YORK
DEPARTMENT OF PERSONNEL
CITY CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION
Medical and Physical Standards for Motor Vehicle Operator—Examination No. 0417
The following shall be contributory causes for rejection:
1. ALCOHOL—Excessive use of.
2. ALLERGY—Severe allergies resulting in skin eruptions, asthma, hay fever, etc.
3. BACK INJURY—History of, with recurrence within the last five years.
4. BLOOD PRESSURE—Systolic higher than 160 or diastolic higher than 90. History of continued treatment for high blood pressure.
5. CHEST X-RAY—Will be required of all applicants. Presence of active tuberculosis or other serious condition revealed by X-Ray.
6. COLOR VISION—Defective perception of red, green and yellow.
7. DEFORMITIES—Any deformities of hands, feet or spine which in the opinion of the Examining Physician would prevent satisfactory driving.
8. DIABETES MELLITUS—History of.
9. DRUGS—Excessive use of, such as the amphetamines, barbiturates or tranquilizers or use of narcotics.
10. EPILEPSY, fainting spells, blackouts or convulsions—History of.
11. GENITO-URINARY—Presence of lesions, discharge, orchitis, varicocele or hypertrophy of prostate gland may reject.
12. HEARING—Inability to hear whispered voice with either ear. Use of hearing aid is not allowed. Presence of any disease interfering with hearing.
13. HEART—Significant cardiac abnormality. Tachycardia, a rate of more than 100 after a period of rest. History of heart disease.
14. HEIGHT—For appointment to Police Department, 5 feet 7 inches in bare feet is required.
15. HERNIA—Presence of until corrected. Use of truss is not allowed.
16. MENTAL HOSPITAL CONFINEMENT OF PSYCHIATRIC CARE—History of, will require psychiatric examination in order to determine employability.
17. NEUROLOGICAL CONDITION or MUSCULAR CONDITION—Any such condition involving weakness or paralysis of extremities.
18. STRENGTH—Inability to lift a 35-pound weight to a full arm's length overhead with one hand and a 30-pound weight with the other, one at a time.
19. ULCER—History of treatment for peptic, gastric or duodenal ulcer within the past 5 years.
20. VARICOSE VEINS—Presence of enlarged, until corrected.
21. VENERAL DISEASE—Presence of.
22. VISION—Minimum of 20/30 vision, with each eye separately, eyeglasses allowed. Minimum of 20/100 with each eye without eyeglasses. No chronic inflammation of lids or conjunctiva or presence of any disease interfering with vision.
23. ANY DISEASE, INJURY or ABNORMALITY, which in the opinion of the Examining Physician tends to impair health or prevent performance of duties.
The following shall be contributory causes for rejection i.e., each one by itself shall not be a cause for rejection:
1. Chronic cough.
2. Cholesterol level over 260 mg.
3. Excessive weight.
Prior to appointment, applicants may be given a Blood Pressure examination, Urinalysis, Wasserman, Hematocrit and, where indicated, additional tests such as Blood Glucose, Blood Cholesterol or Electrocardiogram.
Dated, April 14, 1964.
CITY CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION, TWO-
DORNE H. LANG, Chairman; GEORGE GREGORY, Jr.,
and ANTHONY MAURIZELLO, Commissioners.
FRANK A. SCHAEFER, Secretary.
