CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N. Y.
October 23, 1964
EXECUTIVE ORDER #124
TO: CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR
SUBJECT: ATTENDANCE AT LEGISLATIVE MEETINGS AND CONFERENCES
No City department or agency shall permit a representative to attend or participate in any meeting, conference or discussion pertaining to State legislation with a State official, member of the State Legislature, a Legislative Committee or State agency unless notification is given to, and approval is received from Paul E. Bragdon, Assistant to the Mayor, prior to attendance at such meeting.
There will be absolutely no exceptions to this rule.
ROBERT F. WAGNER
Mayor
