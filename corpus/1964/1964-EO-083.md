CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N. Y.
February 11, 1964
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 83
TO: THE HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR
SUBJECT: PROCESSING OF 1963-1964 EXPENSE BUDGET MODIFICATION REQUESTS
In order to facilitate preparation of the Executive Budget for the fiscal year 1964-1965 modifications of the 1963-1964 expense budget received in this office after March 1, 1964 will not be processed until after March 16, 1964, and will be effective subsequent to this date.
Pending modifications of the 1963-1964 expense budget which are not processed by March 1, 1964, will not be processed until after March 16, 1964.
ROBERT F. WAGNER
MAYOR
