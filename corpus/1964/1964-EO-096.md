CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.

May 27, 1964

## EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 96

TO: APPELLATE DIVISION, FIRST DEPARTMENT AND FIRST JUDICIAL DISTRICT RETIREMENT FUND

FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR

SUBJECT: PENSIONS-PROVIDING-FOR-INCREASED-TAKE-HOME-PAY PLAN FOR MEMBERS OF THE APPELLATE DIVISION, FIRST DEPARTMENT AND FIRST JUDICIAL DISTRICT RETIREMENT FUND FOR THE ONE-YEAR PERIOD BEGINNING JULY 1, 1964

1. Pursuant to the provisions of subd. 8 of Section 108 of the Judiciary Law, as added by L. 1964, c. 638, the Mayor elects to provide that the deduction from the salary or compensation of any employee or officer made pursuant to Section 108 of the Judiciary Law, need not be made and that no contribution in lieu thereof need be made during the one year period commencing with July first, nineteen hundred sixty four.

2. The provisions of this executive order shall not imply any obligation, commitment or promise that benefits herein granted or provided for shall be continued beyond the termination date thereof, as herein prescribed.

ROBERT F. WAGNER
MAYOR
