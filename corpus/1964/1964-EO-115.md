CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.
July 1, 1964
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 115
TO: NEW YORK ZOOLOGICAL SOCIETY
FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER
MAYOR
TO: SUBJECT: ADMISSION OF INSTITUTIONS TO CULTURAL INSTITUTIONS RETIREMENT SYSTEM
Section 1, Subdivision (6) of The Cultural Institutions for the Retired and Disposed of the City of New York provides that, with the approval of the Committee, any cultural institution as defined in the By-Laws receiving financial support from The City of New York may participate in the System.
The New York Zoological Society has applied for membership in the System effective July 1, 1964 and the Committee approved their application for entry at its meeting held on June 29, 1964.
In order to make the application fully effective, I hereby approve admission of this institution to the Retirement System, in accordance with the terms approved by the Pension Committee.
ROBERT F. WAGNER
MAYOR
