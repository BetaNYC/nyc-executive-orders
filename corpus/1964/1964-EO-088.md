CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N. Y.
April 20, 1964
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 88
TO: HEADS OF ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS, COURTS AND AGENCIES TRANSFERRING TO NEW CENTREX TELEPHONE SYSTEM.
In July of 1962, My Executive Order announced the installation of a unified direct dialing system known as Centrex. Since that time 31irty-six departments have been cut over to this system. In June of this year, the second phase of the Centrex installation will be cut over to serve many more City departments and agencies.
To insure the complete effectiveness of the system, it is essential that each telephone user learn about the new telephone system.
The Department of Public Works has been assigned the responsibility of preparing a schedule of instructions for all employees in the Phase II operation not over. Lectures will be conducted by the Telephone Company on the operation of this new equipment. Each lecture will last about one hour and all of the personnel in these departments must attend to insure the complete success of this change in our communication system.
A schedule of dates and the time for each department to attend to receive course of instruction is attached. Please issue the necessary orders to your employees to attend.
Very truly yours,
ROBERT F. WAGNER
MAYOR
