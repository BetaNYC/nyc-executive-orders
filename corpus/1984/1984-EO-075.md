THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 75
April 30, 1984
Amendment of Executive Order
No. 59 (July 27, 1981)
Industrial Loft Advisory Council
Section 1. Prior Order Amended. Paragraph (a) of Section 2 of prior Executive Order No. 59, dated July 27, 1981, is amended to delete any reference to the "Commissioner of the Office of Economic Development," and substitute therefore "Commissioner of the Office of Business Development," and shall read as follows:
"§ 2. Members.
(a) The Council shall be composed of not less than 11 and not more than 15 members. The Commissioner of the Office of Business Development (the "Commissioner") shall be a member of the Council, but shall not be entitled to vote. The other members of the Council shall be appointed by the Mayor."
Section 2. Effective Date. This Order shall be effective immediately.
