# CITY OF NEW YORK

OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N. Y.

May 1, 1947

MEMORANDUM NO. 20

TO: Heads of all Departments including Independent Agencies and Borough Presidents

Please submit to this office the total number of employee bulletin boards in your department.

This information is requested so that sufficient copies may be furnished you of any notices which are sent out from City Hall with the request that they be posted on your bulletin boards.
