# CORRECTED MEMO

August 19, 1947

MEMORANDUM NO. 28

TO: HEADS OF ALL MAYOR'S DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

The American Legion Auxiliary will hold its annual convention in New York City from August 28th to August 31st, 1947.

I desire that all employees who are members of the American Legion Auxiliary who wish to attend the convention be granted leaves of absence with pay on these dates.

William O'Dwyer
Mayor
