THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 2/
MARCH 24, 1995
IMPLEMENTATION OF LOCAL SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT PLAN
PROJECTS
By the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, it is
hereby ordered:
Section 1: The First Deputy Mayor, whose office is established pursuant to Executive Order No. 17, dated January 3, 1995, is hereby authorized to sign applications to secure grant funds, submitted pursuant to provisions of the New York State Environmental Conservation Law or the rules or regulations of the State Department of Environmental Conservation, and to sign any other documents as may be necessary to secure such funds.
Section 2: This Order shall take effect immediately.
RUDOLPH W. GIULIANI
MAYOR
