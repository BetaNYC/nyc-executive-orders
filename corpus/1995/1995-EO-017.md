EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 17
January 3, 1995
AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 2,
DATED JANUARY 1, 1994, RELATING TO
DEPUTY MAYORS
By the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, it is hereby ordered:
Section 1. Section 1 of Executive Order No. 2, dated January 1, 1994, as such Order was amended by Executive Order No. 9, dated June 17, 1994, and Executive Order No. 16, dated December 27, 1994, is amended to read as follows:
Section 1. There shall be four Deputy Mayors. One shall be designated the First Deputy Mayor, one shall be designated the Deputy Mayor for Education and Human Services, one shall be designated the Deputy Mayor for Planning and Community Relations, and one shall be designated the Deputy Mayor for Finance and Economic Development.
