MAYOR'S INVESTIGATORY COMMISSION ON SCHOOL SAFETY
WHEREAS, the safety and well-being of the children in the public schools of the City are of paramount importance; and
WHEREAS, at the same time that the number of reported crimes in major crime categories has declined throughout the City, the New York City schools have continued to experience an alarming incidence of crime and disciplinary problems involving students, teachers and school property, including the use and possession of deadly weapons and other serious offenses; and
WHEREAS, recent incidents involving alleged criminal activity and other misconduct by school Safety Officers have called into question the adequacy of the policies, practices and procedures governing hiring, training, supervision and discipline in the Division of School Safety; and
WHEREAS, questions have been raised as to the adequacy of the Division of School Safety's reporting on the level of criminal activity and other misconduct in the City's schools; and

WHEREAS, the Mayor, as Chief Executive Officer of the City, wants to ensure that the welfare of the City's children is protected to the fullest extent possible; and
WHEREAS, it is necessary to establish a Commission to investigate promptly the effectiveness and adequacy of the present system employed by the Board of Education to provide for school safety, and in order to carry out such review, it is necessary that the Commission have the ability to investigate and gather information to the fullest extent possible, including the ability to request the Commissioner of Investigation to issue subpoenas where necessary;
NOW, THEREFORE, by the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, it hereby is ordered that:
Section 1. Establishment of Investigatory Commission.
There hereby is established an Investigatory Commission on School Safety (the "Commission"), which shall consist of five members appointed by the Mayor, who shall be residents of the City of New York or shall maintain a place of business in the City of New York. Each of the members shall serve without compensation. The Mayor shall appoint a Chairperson from among the members.
Section 2. Powers and Duties.
a. The Commission shall investigate the actual level of criminal activity and other misconduct in the City's schools, and further shall investigate the adequacy of the existing organization, practices, and policies of the school safety operations of the City School District, including the Division of School Safety of the Board of Education, in maintaining safety in

In carrying out its mandate, the Commission may hold public hearings, and shall obtain all information and data necessary to prepare the report required by subdivision (b) of this section, including information from (1) the City School District, including the Board of Education, the Chancellor, the Community School Boards, the Community Superintendents, the Division of School Safety, and any other officer or employee of the school district, (2) the Police Department, and (3) any other law enforcement agencies having knowledge or information about school safety issues generally or with respect to specific cases, to the extent allowable by law.
