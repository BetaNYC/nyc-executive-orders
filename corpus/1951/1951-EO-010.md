CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.
July 20, 1951.
MEMORANDUM No. 10
TO HEADS OF ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES:
The New York Department of the American
Legion will hold its Annual State Convention in
New York City on August 9, 10, 11, 1951.
I desire that all employees who are
members of the American Legion and who wish to
participate in the Parade Saturday, August 11,
1951, be excused from duty.
Vincent R. Impellitteri
Mayor
