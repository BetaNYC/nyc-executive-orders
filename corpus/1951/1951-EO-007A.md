CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.

July 16th, 1951.

MEMORANDUM NO. 7-A

To: The Heads of All City Departments and Agencies

From: The Mayor

In order to consolidate the necessary record keeping and expedite priority assistance for maintenance, repair, and operating supplies involving critical materials for all City agencies, I am amending Memorandum No. 7, originally issued June 13, 1951, as follows: The Department of Purchase will handle, in addition to their present responsibilities for the procurement of supplies, materials and equipment, priority assistance for the critical material necessary in those labor and material contracts for maintenance or repair of existing facilities except when they are concerned with new construction or alterations.
