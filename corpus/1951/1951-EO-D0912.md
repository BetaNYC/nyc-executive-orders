OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
New York 7, N. Y.
September 12, 1951
Dear ----------:
Attached hereto is a copy of the report on city-owned passenger cars made to me by the Board of Management Improvement, together with a directive sent by me to city departments and agencies under my jurisdiction.
These documents are forwarded to you for your information and guidance.
