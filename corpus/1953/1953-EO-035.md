# CITY OF NEW YORK

OFFICE OF THE MAYOR

NEW YORK 7, N. Y.

June 2, 1953.

EXECUTIVE MEMORANDUM No. 35

FROM: MAYOR VINCENT R. IMPELLITTERI

TO: HEADS OF ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

This year the annual Mayor's Trophy Game between the Yankees and Dodgers will be played on Monday evening, June 29, at the Yankee Stadium. Proceeds will again be spent on equipment for the City's young sandlot baseball players.

Last year, despite bad weather, more than 50,000 people attended the game, and the previous year more than 72,000 attended it. The net available for distribution from these two games was $165,000. We anticipate another heavy demand for tickets this year. I ask you to notify the employees of your department that reserved seats will go on sale in Room 1, City Hall, (Col. Edward C. Riekert's office) June 2. This location has been chosen largely for their convenience. I know that many of them will want to see the game and support the excellent cause for which it is played.

Ticket prices (all reserved seats): $1.75, $2.00, $2.50, and $3.00.

PLEASE CIRCULATE THIS MEMORANDUM.
