# CITY OF NEW YORK

OFFICE OF THE MAYOR

NEW YORK 7, N. Y.

June 16, 1952

MEMORANDUM NO. 22

TO ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

This year the annual Mayor's Trophy Game between the Yankees and Dodgers will be played on Monday evening, July 21, at the Yankee Stadium.

Proceeds will again be spent on equipment for the City's young sandlot baseball players.

Last year, 72,729 people attended the game. This set a new record in baseball attendance at a benefit game. The net available for distribution was $91,298.33. We anticipate another heavy demand for tickets. I ask you to notify the employees of your department that reserved seats will go on sale in Room 1, City Hall, (Col. Edward G. Riekert's office) June 20. This location has been chosen largely for their convenience.

I know that many of them will want to see the game and support the cause for which it is played.

PLEASE CIRCULATE THIS MEMORANDUM.

Ticket prices (all reserved) $1.75, $2.00, $2.50, and $3.00.
