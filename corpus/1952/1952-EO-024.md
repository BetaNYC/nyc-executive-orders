August 7, 1952
MEMORANDUM NO. 24
TO ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES:
On October 18, 1952, the National Safety Council will sponsor what promises to be one of the greatest parades in the history of our City -- the New York Green Cross Safety Parade -- which will proceed along Fifth Avenue from 95th Street to 62nd Street. Because of the importance of this event I am asking all City departments and agencies to cooperate with the New York Green Cross in making it a success. You will be hearing from parade director Demetrios Sazani regarding his plans for organization, and I ask that you give him your assistance.
