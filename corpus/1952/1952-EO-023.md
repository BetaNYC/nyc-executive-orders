# CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N. Y.
July 8, 1952
MEMORANDUM NO. 23
TO ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
This is a follow-up to Memorandum No. 22 dated June 16, 1952, wherein I announced the Mayor's Trophy Baseball Game between the Yankees and the Dodgers to be held Monday evening, July 21st, at the Yankee Stadium.
This is a most deserving community project from which all the proceeds will go for the City's young sandlot baseball players. It merits the support of every New Yorker.
Time is growing short and choice tickets are going fast. Please urge your employees who expect to attend this game to purchase their tickets immediately.
They are available in ROOM 1, City Hall, (Edward G. Riekert's office).
Ticket prices (all reserved) $1.75, $2.00, $2.50 and $3.00.
PLEASE CIRCULATE THIS MEMORANDUM TO ALL EMPLOYEES OF YOUR DEPARTMENT.
