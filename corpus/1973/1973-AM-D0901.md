New proposals not introduced at the 1973 session must be sub-
mitted to me by October 1st in brief summary form so that a policy
ion can be made as to whether a proposal should be advanced to the
drafting stage. This summary should include:
1. purpose of the bill;
2. legislative history indicating whether the proposal

has been previously introduced and the action taken, together with copies of such previously introduced legislation;
fiscal impact, if any, and
whether the proposal will require initial drafting by the Corporation Counsel's office.
The legislative proposals which are submitted will be reviewed and the Mayor's Legislative Coordinating Committee and the various departments and agencies will thereafter be notified as to which proposals are acceptable as part of the City's program. When necessary, representatives of the affected City agencies will meet with the Legislative Co-ordinating Committee prior to any policy decision by the Committee. The legislation to the various departments and agencies will also contain further information relating to the pre-filing procedure which, as you know, will be in effect on November 15th.
The Corporation Counsel's Office should be advised no later than November 1st, 1973 as to all approved legislative proposals which will require initial drafting by that office.
Drafts of all legislation prepared by the various departments and agencies should be submitted to me no later than November 15, 1973 after final review by the Corporation Counsel's Office and the Legislative Co-ordinating Committee.
