OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 39
DECEMBER 28, 1979
REVOCATION OF EXECUTIVE ORDER
By the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York,
it is hereby ordered:
Section 1. Prior Order Revoked. Executive Order No. 9 (1970)
is hereby revoked.
§2. Effective Date. This Order shall take effect immediately.
