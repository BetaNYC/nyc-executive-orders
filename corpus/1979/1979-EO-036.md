THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 36
NOVEMBER 7, 1979
REVOCATION OF EXECUTIVE ORDERS
By the power vested in me as Mayor of the
City of New York, it is hereby ordered:
Section 1. Prior Orders Revoked. Executive
Orders Number 94 and Number 97 (1977) are hereby
revoked.
§ 2. Effective Date. This Order shall take
effect immediately.
