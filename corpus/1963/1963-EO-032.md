CITY OF NEW YORK
CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N. Y.
April 4, 1963
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 32
TO: HEADS OF ALL CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR
SUBJECT: VACATE ORDERS
In pursuance of subdivision one of Section 1160-1.0 of the Administrative Code of the City of New York, the head of any agency issuing a vacate order requiring residential tenants to evacuate a building, shall notify the Commissioner of Relocation so that tenants affected may be properly relocated.
Wherever possible, such notification shall provide sufficient time before the effective date of the vacate order to enable the Department of Relocation to carry out its responsibility.
