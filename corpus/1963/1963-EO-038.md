CITY OF NEW YORK
CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.
May 16, 1963
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 38
TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR
SUBJECT: RE: CAPITAL BUDGET AMENDMENTS
All requests by the head of any agency for
amendments to the Capital Budget shall not be submitted
to the City Planning Commission until submitted to the
Office of the Mayor in the first instance for consideration.
ROBERT F. WAGNER
Mayor
