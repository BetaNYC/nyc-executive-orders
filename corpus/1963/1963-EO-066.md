CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.
July 9, 1963
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 66
TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR
SUBJECT: CHANGES AND CORRECTIONS IN EXECUTIVE ORDERS 58 and 59.
I. In order to provide sufficient time for agencies to make promotions of employees without the loss of the July 1st increment date, the following change in Executive Order 58 is approved:
Change the date on line 3 of paragraph one to read "August 23, 1963."
II. To correct a typographical error in line 10 of paragraph thirteen (a) of Executive Order 59 the following change is approved:
Change "Board of Estimate" to read "Mayor."
III. To clarify the provisions of paragraph two (a) of Executive Order 59 the following addition is approved to be inserted on line 3, after Sanitation services; "or experts, consultants etc."
IV. To clarify the provisions of paragraph two (a) of Executive Order 59 the following addition is approved to be inserted on line 5, after regulations:
"or if a former employee is reinstated he may be granted the salary previously attained."
V. To clarify the provision of paragraph two (b) of Executive Order 59 the following addition is approved, to be inserted on line 3 after Mayor:
"Reinstatement of former employees may be made by an agency head at the salary previously attained by the employee."
ROBERT F. WAGNER
MAYOR
