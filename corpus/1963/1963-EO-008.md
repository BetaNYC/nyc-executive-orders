NEW YORK 7, N.Y.

DEPUTY MAYOR

March 6, 1962 g

EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 8

TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

FROM: EDW. F. CAVANAGH, JR., DEPUTY MAYOR

SUBJECT: MAYOR'S MEDAL FOR OUTSTANDING CITY EMPLOYEES

You are cordially invited to attend the presentation of the Mayor's Medal for Outstanding City Employees by Mayor Wagner in a ceremony at City Hall on Thursday, March 15th, at 3:30 P.M.

This will mark the first such presentation. The Medal was established through Executive Order #100 of 1961 and is given annually to each of the two employees of the City whose work merits the highest recognition. Would you please inform your staff of this ceremony.

Edw. F. Cavanagh, Jr.
Deputy Mayor
