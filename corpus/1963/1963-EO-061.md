CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.

June 28, 1963

EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 61

TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR

SUBJECT: SPECIAL SCHEDULES, CAPITAL BUDGET PROJECTS

Certain appropriations in the Capital Budget for 1963 contain amounts for engineering and other services as part of the project cost. Schedules of these amounts, are hereby approved. Effective July 1, 1963.

ROBERT F. WAGNER
MAYOR
