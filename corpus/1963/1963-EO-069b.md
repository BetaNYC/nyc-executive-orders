CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
September 9, 1963
EXECUTIVE ORDER #69
Thirty-five copies of each proposed bill and supporting memorandum, are to be submitted to Bernard J. Ruggieri, Assistant to the Mayor, as soon as possible.
Those agencies and department which require technical assistance should promptly communicate with the Law Department so that the above schedule may be adhered to.
Your cooperation in compliance with the terms of this directive is anticipated in order to obtain maximum results in the formulation, preparation and processing of the City's 1964 Legislative Program.
ROBERT F. WAGNER
Mayor
