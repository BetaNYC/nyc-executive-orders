CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N. Y.
PAUL R. SCREVANE
DEPUTY MAYOR
June 5, 1961
EXECUTIVE ORDER # 94
TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND MAYORAL AGENCIES
FROM: DEPUTY MAYOR
SUBJECT: COMMUNICATION BY PHONE BETWEEN THE MAYOR'S OFFICE AND DEPARTMENT HEADS.
For the further improvement of communications, you are requested to provide this office with names, titles, office and home telephone numbers of staff members who, in your absence, can be contacted both during and after working hours when such is deemed necessary by the Mayor's Office. This list should include executive staff such as Deputy, Assistant, Secretary, Public Relations Officer, and should be sent without delay to the Office of the Mayor.
