CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.

March 10, 1961

EXECUTIVE MEMORANDUM #88

TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: ROBERT F. WAGNER, MAYOR

SUBJECT: CENTRAL REGISTRY FOR MULTIPLE DWELLING VIOLATIONS

A central registry for multiple dwelling violations
is hereby established in the Department of Buildings.

All departments are directed to forward, after May 1, 1961,
a duplicate copy of violation notices affecting multiple dwellings to
the Department of Buildings' office in the borough in which the multiple
dwelling is located within three working days from the date of issuance
of such notice, and to advise the same office as soon as the violations
have been dismissed. Violation orders resulting in immediate
on-the-spot compliance need not be forwarded to the central violations
registry.

ROBERT F. WAGNER
Mayor
