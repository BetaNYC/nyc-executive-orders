CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.
December 1st, 1961
EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 104
TO ALL MAYOR'S DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
All appointees holding positions in the unclassified or exempt services, except those holding appointments for specific terms, will submit their resignations not later than 12 o'clock noon, December 15th, 1961, to take effect December 31st, 1961.
Reappointments will be announced some time before January 1st, 1962.
ROBERT F. WAGNER
Mayor
