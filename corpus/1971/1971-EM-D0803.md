THE CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N.Y. 10007
# MEMORANDUM
August 3, 1971
TO: HEADS OF ALL ADMINISTRATIONS AND DEPARTMENTS
FROM: DEPUTY MAYOR RICHARD R. AURELIO
On July 29, Mayor Lindsay issued an Executive Memorandum announcing the 1971 United Fund Campaign for all Municipal employees.
The Mayor has given the United Fund his complete endorsement and has appointed me as the Co-Chairman of the Campaign. In the Memorandum, the Mayor has asked the heads of each Administration and Department to appoint a senior member of their executive staff to serve as Campaign Chairman and a representative of their major employee union to serve as Co-Chairman. Please have Chairmen's names in my office by August 12.
I have asked Mr. Stephen Briganti, Division Director of the United Fund, to contact you for an appointment. He will meet with you concerning the campaign and will need only a few minutes of your time to brief you on what is necessary to run an effective campaign.
Please lend your complete support in helping us make this a most successful campaign.
