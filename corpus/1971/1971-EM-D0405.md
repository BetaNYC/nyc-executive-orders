# MEMORANDUM

TO: Agency Heads

FROM: Richard R. Aurelio
Deputy Mayor

April 5, 1971

In line with my memorandum of April 1, I am sending you a copy of a letter I have sent to Manhattan Borough President Percy Sutton and Councilwoman Carol Greitzer on the City's efforts to insure equal employment opportunities and public service.

Any problems that may arise in your agency regarding the enforcement of the Law Against Discrimination and the Mayor's Executive Orders on equal opportunity should be discussed with Mrs. Eleanor Holmes Norton, Chairman of the City's Commission on Human Rights.
