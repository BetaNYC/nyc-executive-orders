EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 45
NOVEMBER 13, 1975
ELECTRICAL ENERGY SAVINGS
Whereas, The City of New York is currently in a fiscal crisis which mandates curtailment programs in areas where fiscal economies may be realized; and
Whereas, The costs of electrical energy for Mayoral agencies are appropriated in the Municipal Service Administration Budget; and
Whereas, The Municipal Service Administration has demonstrated financial economies in a program designed to effect substantial electrical energy savings in its Administration; and
Whereas, There exists a need to expand these electrical energy savings to apply to all Mayoral agencies,
Now, therefore, by the power vested in me as Mayor of The City of New York, it is hereby ordered:
Section 1. The Municipal Service Administrator is hereby charged with the duty and responsibility of immediately developing plans, procedures, priorities and policies for achieving the proper, efficient and most economical use of electrical energy in all Mayoral agencies of The City of New York and shall promulgate rules, regulations and orders for the implementation thereof. The Administrator is further vested with the power and charged with the duty of enforcing and implementing as expeditiously as possible any such rules, regulations and orders.
§ 2. All Mayoral departments and agencies of the City are directed to fully cooperate, assist and immediately implement all policies and orders of the Municipal Service Administrator or his designated representative pursuant to this Executive Order.
§ 3. The Municipal Service Administrator shall render periodic reports to the Mayor indicating the policies, implementation, effectiveness and resulting savings achieved as a result of this program.
§ 4. All governmental agencies not covered by this Executive Order are invited to cooperate with the Municipal Service Administrator and to participate in any plans, procedures, priorities, and policies developed as a result of this Order.
§ 5. With respect to electrical energy programs for savings, this Order augments Executive Order No. 29, dated April 16, 1975, and grants specific powers, duties and responsibilities to the Municipal Service Administrator.
§ 6. This Order shall take effect immediately.
