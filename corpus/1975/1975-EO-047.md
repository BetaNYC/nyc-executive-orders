EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 47
NOVEMBER 26, 1975
BOARD OF ADVISORS FOR SAILORS' SNUG HARBOR
Whereas, By purchase on September 22, 1972 and November 7, 1974, The City of New York acquired for park purposes a tract of land of approximately 80 acres in the Borough of Staten Island, known as Sailors' Snug Harbor;
Whereas, Sailors' Snug Harbor temporarily remains occupied by its former owners, the institution for retired seamen, which will be relocated to new facilities, after which the property will be available for park, recreational and cultural facility is required to be developed;
Whereas, Sailors' Snug Harbor is a park, recreational and cultural resource of great significance for the Borough of Staten Island;
Whereas, It is necessary and appropriate that there be participation by the citizens of the Borough of Staten Island in the planning for and maintenance of the development of Sailors' Snug Harbor.
Now, therefore, by the power vested in me as Mayor of The City of New York, it is hereunder ceded to all interested parties that the City of New York, in its capacity as the Administrator of Parks, Recreation and Cultural Affairs, shall, consist of the Administrator of Parks, Recreation and Cultural Affairs, the Director of City Planning and such other persons, not to exceed twenty, as shall be appointed to such Board by the Mayor. The Board shall have a chairman who shall be named by the Mayor.
§ 2. The Board of Advisors shall, in consultation with the Administrator of Parks, Recreation and Cultural Affairs, develop and propose to the Mayor a plan for the use, development, maintenance, management and operation of Sailors' Snug Harbor.
§ 3. The Board shall use all practical means to keep the public informed of its deliberations and before proposing its plan for the Harbor to the Mayor, shall hold a public hearing thereon.
§ 4. The Administrator of Parks, Recreation and Cultural Affairs shall, from time to time, report on the use, development, operation and management of Sailors' Snug Harbor and shall consult with the Board with respect to the same.
§ 5. This Order shall take effect immediately.
ABRAHAM D. BEAMAN, Mayor
