EXECUTIVE ORDER #68

September 9, 1959

Eight copies of each proposed bill, together with eight copies of the supporting memorandum, are to be submitted to Bernard J. Ruggieri, Assistant to the Mayor, as soon as possible.

The final date for submitting proposed legislation for inclusion in the City's 1960 Legislative Program is November 1. Legislation which would affect City or State finances in any way should be submitted immediately.

Your cooperation in compliance with the terms of this directive is anticipated in order to obtain maximum results in the formulation, preparation and processing of the City's 1960 Legislative Program.

ROBERT F. WAGNER
MAYOR
