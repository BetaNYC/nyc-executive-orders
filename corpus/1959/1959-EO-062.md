# CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N. Y.
April 1, 1959
MEMORANDUM NO. 62
TO: ALL MAYOR'S DEPARTMENT AND AGENCY HEADS
FROM: PAUL T. O'KEEFE, DEPUTY MAYOR
In connection with absences without leave of employees on Tuesday, March 31st, should deduction of pay be required under your department's rules and regulations, the regular pay check due April 3rd shall be released and not held up for the preparation of a refund notice. Pay deductions shall be made through the usual procedure on the report of changes of personnel.
PAUL T. O'KEEFE
Deputy Mayor
