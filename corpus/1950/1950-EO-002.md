December 14, 1950 bf

Memorandum No. 2

To: Heads of all City Departments and Agencies

At this season of the year most of the members of your staff will desire an opportunity to do their Christmas shopping.

It is my desire that you arrange that this opportunity be afforded to all who desire it by granting the necessary time off from duty.

Since Christmas Day and New Year's Day fall on a Monday this year, it is my desire that all personnel in your office be given a holiday on Saturday, December 23rd and Saturday, December 30th. This shall apply to all personnel, except those necessary for the proper protection of life, health and property.

Please accept for yourself and extend to all the members of your staff my very best wishes for a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year.

MAYOR
