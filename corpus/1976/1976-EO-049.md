OFFICE OF THE MAYOR

EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 49

JANUARY 22, 1976

DELEGATION OF AUTHORITY TO FIRST DEPUTY MAYOR

Whereas, The City of New York may, from time to time, enter into agreements with the Municipal Assistance Corporation for The City of New York; and

Whereas, John E. Zuccotti, First Deputy Mayor, is fully familiar with the facts necessary to enter into such agreements.

Now, therefore, by virtue of the power vested in me as Mayor and pursuant to Section three of the City Charter, it is hereby ordered as follows:

Section 1. John E. Zuccotti, First Deputy Mayor, is hereby delegated the authority to act for, on behalf of and at the direction of the Mayor in the exercise of all functions, powers and duties which the Mayor may have pursuant to Article 10 of the Public Authorities Law, as amended (the "Act"), including, but not limited to, full power to execute for, on behalf of and at the direction of the Mayor any agreement between the City and the Municipal Assistance Corporation for The City of New York pursuant to Section 3038 of the Act, any certificate pursuant to Section 3037 of the Act and any other document in connection with the Act.

§ 2. The exercise of functions, powers and duties by the First Deputy Mayor for, on behalf of and at the direction of the Mayor pursuant to this Executive Order shall not require the consent in writing of the Mayor or any other officer of the City.

§ 3. Executive Order No. 39 dated September 8, 1975, is hereby revoked, provided, however, that nothing in this Executive Order shall be construed to affect the validity of any act performed pursuant to such Executive Order and prior to the date hereof.

§ 4. This Executive Order shall take effect immediately and remain in effect until revoked by a subsequent Executive Order.

ABRAHAM D. BEAME, Mayor.
