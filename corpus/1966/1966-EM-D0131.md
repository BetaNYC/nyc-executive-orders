CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.
January 31, 1966
66/8
To All Department and Agency Heads
As you know through previous instructions, the Vacancy Control Board has established procedures whereby requests for filling sensitive positions are given priority attention.
There may be cases of extreme emergency, however, where an immediate verbal approval of a sensitive filling is desirable.
Should such emergencies occur during other than normal business hours of the City, please contact the Vacancy Control Board through the Mayor's office, 566-5700.
## The Vacancy Control Board
- Deputy Mayor-City Administrator
- Acting Director of the Budget
- Acting Personnel Director
