# MEMORANDUM
TO: COMMISSIONERS, DEPUTY COMMISSIONERS, HEADS OF CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES, AND CITY HALL STAFF
FROM: JOHN V. LINDSAY FEBRUARY 24, 1966
1. This memorandum revokes Executive Order No. 26 (December 21, 1962).
2. All communications addressed to the Mayor should be delivered to the Correspondence Control Unit (Room 106) where each item will be numbered for identification.
3. Those items requiring a reply by a Department or Agency will be acknowledged over the Mayor's signature and then forwarded to the Department or Agency with a coding sheet attached.
