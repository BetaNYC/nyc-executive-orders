CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.

EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 35

EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 35

December 21, 1966

-4-

(a) Department of Public Works shall reassign 4 laborers and 1 junior engineer, and eliminate 16 motor vehicle operator positions.

(b) Department of Health shall reassign one clerk and eliminate 3 motor vehicle operator positions.

(c) Department of Water Supply, Gas and Electricity shall reassign 7 laborers, and eliminate 10 motor vehicle operator positions.

(d) Department of Sanitation shall reassign one clerk and eliminate one motor vehicle operator position.

(e) Board of Water Supply shall eliminate 3 motor vehicle operator positions.

(f) Department of Correction shall eliminate one motor vehicle operator position.

(g) City Planning Commission shall reassign one motor vehicle operator to the Department of Public Works.

(h) Department of Traffic shall eliminate 4 motor vehicle operator positions.

(i) Department of Buildings shall eliminate 3 motor vehicle operator positions.

(j) Department of Parks shall eliminate 5 motor vehicle operator positions.

(k) Department of Purchase shall eliminate 1 motor vehicle operator position.

(l) Housing and Redevelopment Board shall eliminate 4 motor vehicle operator positions.

(m) Department of Highways shall eliminate 8 motor vehicle operator positions.

(n) Department of Markets shall eliminate one motor vehicle operator position.
