Executive Order No. 6

February 1, 1966

FUNCTION AND COMPOSITION OF NEW YORK CITY COUNCIL
AGAINST POVERTY ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY COMMITTEE,
AND ANTI-POVERTY OPERATIONS BOARD.

This Executive Order modifies
Executive Order No. 160 dated June 30, 1965 to the extent
hereafter set forth:

1. The Anti-Poverty Operations Board shall be composed of the following officials of the City of New York:

(1) Frank Arricale, Executive Director, New York City Youth Board;

(2) Eugene Becker, Acting Director, Bureau of the Budget;

(3) William Booth, Chairman, Commission on Human Rights;

(4) Timothy Costello, Deputy Mayor-City Administrator;

(5) Bernard Donovan, Superintendent of Schools of the Board of Education;

(6) Donald H. Elliott, Counsel to the Mayor;

(7) Barry Gottehrer, Assistant to the Mayor;

(8) Solomon Hoberman, Acting Director, Department of Personnel;

(9) Thomas Hoving, Commissioner, Parks Department;
