CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N. Y. 10007

# MEMORANDUM

TO: COMMISSIONERS, DEPUTY COMMISSIONERS, HEADS OF CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES, AND CITY HALL STAFF

FROM: JOHN V. LINDSAY

JULY 8, 1966

By virtue of the power vested in me as Mayor of the City of New York, I have today formed a committee to be known as "The Task Force on Municipal Archives" under the Chairmanship of Professor Richard B. Morris of Columbia University.

The Task Force is composed of distinguished professors and scholars with great knowledge of our City's history and affairs. I have charged the Chairman and the members of the Task Force with the responsibility of preparing a comprehensive report on the municipal records and archives which will include recommendations for their preservation and utilization.

This is to advise you that I will expect all departments and agencies and the employees therein to cooper-ate fully with the Chairman and members of The Task Force
on Municipal Archives, as well as persons acting with their
authority. It is anticipated that The Task Force will wish to
inspect the records in your custody or under your control,
and will require detailed information about such matters as
microfilm programs, storage arrangements and policies for
record retention, disposal and destruction. This enumeration of Task Force activities is not inclusive. You are
hereby requested to afford such aid and assistance as may
be necessary to facilitate the speedy attainment of its objectives.

JOHN V. LINDSAY
MAYOR
