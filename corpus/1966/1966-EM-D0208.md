CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.
February 8, 1966
MEMORANDUM
TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: JOHN V. LINDSAY, MAYOR
SUBJECT: COMMENTS ON CITY COUNCIL LEGISLATION
Please forward to Robert W. Sweet, Executive Assistant to the Mayor, any comments of your department or agency on local legislation in response to a request by the City Council. He will undertake the submission of such comments to the Council.
