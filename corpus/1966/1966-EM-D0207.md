CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK 7, N.Y.

February 7, 1966

MEMORANDUM

TO: HEADS OF ALL DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES

FROM: JOHN V. LINDSAY, MAYOR

Jim Carberry has the responsibility for working with the Mayor on the drafts of all important speeches, outlining the policies and programs of this administration. When Mr. Carberry calls you for research, facts or drafts from your area of responsibility I would appreciate it if you would give this top priority. Each Department or Agency should designate one person who would be a source of information.
