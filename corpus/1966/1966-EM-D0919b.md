CITY OF NEW YORK
OFFICE OF THE MAYOR
NEW YORK, N. Y. 10007
# MEMORANDUM
September 19, 1966
TO: ALL COMMISSIONERS, HEADS OF AGENCIES, AND ASSISTANTS TO THE MAYOR
FROM: MAYOR JOHN V. LINDSAY
It is most important that all Commissioners inform Mr. Murray Drabkin in the Washington Office when you plan to go to Washington for any reason. Please advise Mr. Drabkin of the date, place and nature of the appearance as well as any other information that may be helpful to the Washington Office.
There are many important and good reasons for this:
First, the Washington Office may be able to give you some information that will assist you.
Second, the press is in constant touch with the Washington Office about the visits to Washington by members of the New York City government and it is important that Mr. Drabkin be able to answer questions if appropriate to do so.
Third, on two occasions to date Mr. Drabkin and the Office have been called as a follow-up to a visit by a New York City official, and it would have been helpful if they had known of the visit in the first place.
Fourth, in connection with any visit to the Executive Branch of the Federal government it may be that there is a Congressional aspect which is good to know about and where we can receive assistance.
