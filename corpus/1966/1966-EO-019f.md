EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. #2
January 10, 1966
TO: CITY DEPARTMENTS AND AGENCIES
FROM: JOHN V. LINDSAY, MAYOR
SUBJECT: ATTENDANCE AT LEGISLATIVE MEETINGS AND CONFERENCES
No City department or agency shall permit a representative to attend or participate in any meeting, conference or discussion pertaining to State legislation with a State official, member of the State Legislature, a Legislative Committee or State Agency unless notification is given to, and approval is received from Richard M. Rosen, Assistant to the Mayor, prior to attendance at such meeting.
There will be no exceptions to this rule.
Mr. Rosen is thoroughly familiar with the State Legislature and its procedures. He formerly served as Executive Assistant to the Attorney General, and was in charge of the Attorney General's legislative program. Please feel free to call upon Mr. Rosen at any time.
MAYOR
