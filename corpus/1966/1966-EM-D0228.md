February 28, 1966

MEMORANDUM

TO: All Commissioners, Heads of Departments and Agencies

FROM: Mayor John V. Lindsay

I am attaching a copy of the press release announcing the appointment of Dr. Efren Ramirez as Narcotics Coordinator for the City of New York.

Following a trip to Europe to deliver a paper in Rome and the cleaning up of his personal affairs in San Juan, Dr. Ramirez will be full time in the City Administrator's office.

I am asking all Commissioners and offices in the City government to give him full cooperation.

Thank you.
