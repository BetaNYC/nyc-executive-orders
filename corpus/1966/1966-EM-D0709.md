## MEMORANDUM
July 9, 1966
TO: Heads of All City Departments and Agencies
FROM: John V. Lindsay, Mayor
On July 8, I appointed Professor Richard B. Morris of Columbia University to serve as Chairman of a recently constituted Task Force on Municipal Archives.
This Task Force will report on the present condition of all records kept by departments and agencies of the City of New York -- executive, legislative and judicial -- and will make recommendations for their safekeeping and for effective utilization.
I am, therefore, requesting your cooperation with the Task Force on Municipal Archives to allow access to authorized persons operating under its authority, to make all records fully available for inspection, and to provide such other information essential to the purposes of the Task Force.
Your cooperation in this effort will be greatly appreciated.
